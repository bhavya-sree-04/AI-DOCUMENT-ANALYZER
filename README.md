# SmartDoc AI – AI Document Intelligence Platform

SmartDoc AI is an AI-powered document intelligence platform designed to analyze and extract meaningful information from various document types, including medical prescriptions, legal documents, resumes, invoices, and business records. The platform leverages AI and document processing technologies to provide intelligent summarization, information extraction, document classification, and conversational document analysis.

## Features

* Medical prescription analysis and information extraction
* Legal document analysis and summarization
* Resume parsing and ATS scoring
* Invoice and business document processing
* AI-powered document chat
* Intelligent document classification
* Secure user authentication
* Document management dashboard

* Extracts structured information from unstructured documents
